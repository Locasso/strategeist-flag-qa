# Strategeist flag QA

28–29 October 2026 · Client review report. Use the flag gallery to inspect every source and tested setting.

## Results

All **1,063 flag PNGs** in `SGAssets/Flags` were tested with the actual `MI_FlagEdit` material. Each source is 152×93 pixels. There are **4,055 Unreal renders** and **1,063 annotated comparison images**.

The visual pass flagged **433 cases**. Another **630** had no strong local clipping, streaking or composition issue by that criterion; those include **98** with a separate numerical edge-blending risk. These are screening results, not certification of zero distortion.

| Group | Flags | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| A · No obvious local issue | 532 | Readable motif at the default; global aspect-ratio error still applies |
| B · Edge / composition issue | 114 | Edge streaks, extension or composition changes |
| C · Motif clipped | 169 | Wider sampling may recover content, at a smaller motif size |
| D · Adapt artwork or UVs | 150 | Clipping combined with pattern/composition problems |
| E · Edge blending risk | 98 | No strong local issue flagged, but opposing edge colors can blend |

Overlapping findings: **319** motif-clipping cases, **152** edge-streaking/extension cases and **248** composition-adaptation cases. Do not add these counts together.

## Scale and distortion

The material samples `clamp((UV - 0.5) * (X,Y) + 0.5, 0, 1)`. UVs beyond the source range repeat its edge sample. A Wrap sampler can also blend opposing source edges at exact bilinear boundaries. The banner silhouette clips side content and lower corners.

For a square brush and a 152×93 source, local aspect-ratio error is `(Y / X) / (152 / 93) - 1`.

| Test | X | Y | Local aspect-ratio error | Tradeoff |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Default | 1.2 | 2.2 | +12.17% | Requested reference; features are relatively wider |
| Aspect ratio | 1.2 | 1.96129 | 0% | Preserves feature proportions; clipping and edge extension remain possible |
| Lower Y | 1.2 | 1.4 | −28.62% | Reduces streaks but stretches features vertically |
| Fit motif | 2 | 3.26882 | 0% | Recovers lateral content, reduces motif size and may worsen edge extension |
| Clamp control | 1.2 | 1.25 | −36.27% | Reduces internal clamping with substantial aspect distortion |

Zero percent refers to local feature scaling **before clamping and silhouette clipping**. No tested alternative is a universal production fix. At the default, the approximate inner window retains 62% of the source width along its centerline.

## Representative findings

- **Abensberg / Arezzo:** readable centered compositions. The aspect-ratio control removes the default widening. Lowering every flag to Y1.4 is unsuitable: it can move Abensberg's emblem into the pointed clipping area.
- **Aragon:** the emblem is left-aligned in the source. The estimated motif mask retains about 84.2% at the default and 100% at X2/Y3.2688. The latter restores the emblem at a smaller size and does not recenter it. Y alone cannot recover horizontal clipping.
- **Antioch:** repeated fleurs-de-lis at source boundaries turn into vertical streaks. Y1.4 and Y1.25 reduce those streaks while changing motif proportions. Preserving both field and symbols requires artwork or UV adaptation.
- **Frankfurt:** the upper-left canton emblem is mostly clipped. Estimated mask retention is about 24.8% at the default; the fit test recovers it at a small size.

## Position and symmetry

Motif position is estimated from color-based segmentation and the center of its bounding box. It is **not semantic recognition**. A candidate mask may contain field regions, ornaments or multiple symbols. Retention estimates use this mask and a conservative approximate banner silhouette; read them alongside the images.

Estimated positions: **761 centered**, **24 left-offset**, **13 right-offset**, and **265 fields/compositions without an isolated motif**. Intrinsic heraldic asymmetry or an animal shown in profile is not a defect. The criterion is information loss or deformation introduced by the material.

The evidence includes 17 source/default contact sheets and ten alternative-setting sheets. A focused visual review of 45 heuristic discrepancies added five clipping cases to the final set.

## Evidence and limits

- Renders use the real Unreal Canvas GPU, a temporary MID parented to the original material, and a 512×512 sRGB target. The audited WBP has one material Image in a 256×256 SizeBox; captures use 2× sampling. This was an isolated material pass, without Play, interaction or multi-DPI validation. Annotated cards are not screenshots of the editor Details panel.
- The legacy `OldTextures/SmallFlags` assets are 33×20. This pass used the requested 152×93 source PNGs as transient textures. Production texture import/compression is not certified.
- The 14 auxiliary `.uasset` files outside Content share names with tested PNGs; they were not counted as additional designs or loaded as standalone packages.
- Presentation PNGs preserve the engine-rendered RGB values. The Canvas capture alpha was discarded for opaque browser display. No geometry, colors or clipping were retouched.
- All four pairs of byte-identical source PNGs produced identical default-render pixels.
- No Unreal asset or source PNG was created, saved or modified during the QA pass.

## Report files

`captures/`: shareable comparisons. `renders/`: per-setting PNGs. `sources/`: source previews. `contact_sheets/`: review sheets. `data/results.json`: client-facing findings and parameters. Internal audit files and scripts are excluded from this hosted report.

Use X1.2/Y1.96129 as an aspect-ratio control for centered motifs. For production, choose between adding artwork padding, supporting individual UV offsets, or treating emblems separately from their backgrounds. A single X/Y pair cannot preserve every horizontal flag while filling the vertical banner without these tradeoffs.
